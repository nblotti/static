    - segment: core_analysis
      description: >
        Deep dive into the main ideas, methodology, or technical

    - segment: implications
      description: >
        Discussion of real-world impact, limitations, trade-offs,

    - segment: closing_reflection
      description: >
        Summarize key insights and leave listeners with a
